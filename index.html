Current Internship

Renewable-Energy CAD & Electrical Design

OurEnergy is a technical consulting, engineering, and project development firm focused on renewable energy. On the engineering team I produce the electrical design documentation behind real solar-plus-storage microgrid installations, from single-line diagrams through code-compliant conductor and grounding design.

  • Drafted single-line diagrams in AutoCAD for 12 microgrid installations, mapping PV array, battery inverter, transfer switch, and utility service connections into a single reviewed drawing set.
  • Sized conductors, conduit, and overcurrent protection for microgrid feeders and combiner runs, verifying every branch stayed under the 3% voltage-drop target and met NEC 690 and 705 requirements.
  • Developed grounding and bonding plans for battery enclosures and PV racking, coordinating equipment ground-conductor sizing with the mechanical enclosure layout.
